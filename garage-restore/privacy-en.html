Garage Restore Privacy Policy

Revision: 24 November 2026. App version: 1.0.4 (110).

1. App and developer

This policy covers only Garage Restore for Android, package com.garagerestore.game, distributed through RuStore. It does not cover Parcel Rush or the developer website. The party responsible for the app and data processing is Emil Zaynullovich Nizamov (Низамов Эмиль Зайнуллович), Garage Restore Studio. Privacy contact: enizamov151@gmail.com. Website: https://enizamov10-hub.github.io/.

2. Data held by the game

Playing does not require registration or entering a name, phone number or email address. Levels, cars, currency, upgrades, inventory, Premium status and settings are stored on the device. The personalization choice and advertising reward and victory counters are stored there too. Our game code does not send these values to the advertising SDK and does not implement its own cloud saves. Any system backup depends on Android and device settings.

3. Advertising SDK and data

The game uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK for Unity 8.7.0, including AppMetrica dependencies. The SDK may process an IP address, technical device and app information, advertising and service identifiers, and information about ad loading, display, dismissal and interaction. Identifier availability depends on Android, device services and user choices. AppMetrica included with the advertising SDK may process technical diagnostics. This description reflects the build and supplier documentation; it does not mean every field is sent in every request.

4. Advertising purposes and choices

Advertising processing supports ad delivery and measurement, reward confirmation and operation of the advertising service. Rewards require SDK confirmation. Interstitial ads may appear after three victories, outside a level; Premium excludes forced displays. No banners are used.

Personalization is off by default. You can allow or disable it under Settings → Ads and privacy. The choice is saved and passed to the SDK at launch. Declining personalization does not prevent play and does not eliminate technical SDK network requests. Where personalization requires consent, merely using the game is not treated as that consent.

5. Location and data access

SDK location sharing is disabled. The app does not request Android access to precise or approximate location, camera, microphone, contacts or SMS. It does not send a name, email, age or gender to the SDK. An IP address is required for network communication and may let the provider estimate a region; this differs from transmitting device coordinates. The advertising SDK includes Android's AD_ID permission. You can additionally restrict advertising ID availability in Android settings.

6. Recipients, retention and safeguards

Advertising data goes to Yandex and services needed for its advertising infrastructure. Yandex's policy and AppMetrica documentation linked below describe processing, possible international transfers, retention and safeguards. We do not set or promise a separate deletion period for the provider's servers. Local data remains until reset or clearing app data; deleting local data does not automatically delete information previously sent to a provider. Technical data is not necessarily fully anonymous.

7. Requests and rights

You can change your personalization choice in the game. Subject to applicable law, you may request information, access, correction, erasure or restriction, object to processing and withdraw consent. Use the developer contact above; Yandex's policy also provides ways to contact it about its data. Do not send passwords or payment details. If a request concerns a child's data, mention that in your request. You retain the right to complain to the competent authority.

8. Changes

This policy is updated if the SDK or processing practices change. The revision date appears at the beginning of this document. Where separate consent is required, changing this text does not replace obtaining it.
